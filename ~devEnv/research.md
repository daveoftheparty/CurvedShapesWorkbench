I think, the info on the soft limitation on how the bounding curves to be drawn should be in the documentation of the WB. The requirement on following the XY-XZ-YZ planes makes people think that a fully perpendicular drawing is the best, while it is better if the two are lined up roughly.

I was trying to draw a wingtip for an RC plane with a 90 deg curvature and I could not figure out what was the problem. But rotating the two bounding curves closer to each other as MangoJelly suggested did the magic.
